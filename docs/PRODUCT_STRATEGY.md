# Product Strategy

## Positioning

Benbax should compete on reliability, address clarity, rider trust, and operational speed. The product should feel close to Uber/Bolt in simplicity and Glovo in delivery coverage, with Ghana-specific addressing as a differentiator.

## Supported Services

- Food delivery.
- Parcel delivery.
- Courier services.
- Grocery and pharmacy delivery.
- Future transport scalability through the `TRANSPORT_READY` category.

## Differentiators

- Landmark-first Ghana addressing.
- Voice-note directions and WhatsApp location support.
- OTP and proof-based delivery verification.
- Smart rider assignment using location freshness and performance.
- Offline queue for weak internet.
- Admin risk dashboard for fraud and safety.
- Hybrid delivery model that can expand into ride-hailing.

## MVP Launch

1. Customer registration, delivery quote, delivery creation, live tracking.
2. Rider onboarding, KYC submission, availability, dispatch offer, completion.
3. Admin monitoring, manual dispatch trigger, rider/user management basics.
4. MTN MoMo payment initialization and cash-on-delivery.
5. Push notifications and emergency support.

## Commercial Metrics

- Delivery completion rate.
- Average assignment time.
- Average ETA accuracy.
- Rider acceptance rate.
- Support ticket rate per 100 deliveries.
- Repeat customer rate.
- Contribution margin per delivery.
- Fraud loss rate.
