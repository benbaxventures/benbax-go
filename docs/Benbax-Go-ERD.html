Entity Catalog And Relationship Matrix

The schema is optimized around fast delivery creation, rider matching, live GPS, payment reconciliation, customer support, and operational control.

Cardinality 1:1, 1:0..1, 1:N Delete model Mostly cascade on dependent records Scale path PostGIS and tracking partitions

Identity and access

User, RefreshToken, DeviceToken, Notification, and AuditLog support customer, rider, admin, operations, and support roles.

Delivery lifecycle

Delivery owns stops, assignments, tracking points, payment, proof, chat, support links, emergency incidents, and risk flags.

Rider operations

RiderProfile links a user to vehicle, KYC documents, availability, location, assignments, tracking points, and suspicious activity.

Money movement

Payment captures delivery charges. Wallet and WalletTransaction handle customer balances, refunds, rider payouts, and incentive accounting.

Primary Relationships

Parent Child Cardinality Purpose
User Delivery 1:N A customer can request many deliveries. Each delivery has one customer.
User RiderProfile 1:0..1 A rider account extends the base user profile with KYC, location, and dispatch data.
RiderProfile DeliveryAssignment 1:N Riders receive dispatch offers with score, response state, and expiry.
Delivery DeliveryAssignment 1:N A delivery can have multiple offers over time until one is accepted.
Delivery DeliveryTrackingPoint 1:N Live GPS stream and historical route replay.
RiderProfile DeliveryTrackingPoint 1:N Each tracking point belongs to the rider who produced it.
Delivery Payment 1:0..1 One active charge record per delivery for MoMo, Paystack, wallet, or COD.
Delivery DeliveryProof 1:0..1 OTP, proof photo, signature, and final verification metadata.
User SavedLocation 1:N Localized Ghana addresses with landmarks, voice notes, and WhatsApp pins.
Delivery SupportTicket / EmergencyIncident / SuspiciousActivity 1:N Operational controls for complaints, emergencies, and fraud investigation.
